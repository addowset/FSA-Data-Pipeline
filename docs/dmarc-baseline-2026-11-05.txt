DMARC baseline for opendo.co.uk, taken 5 November 2026
=======================================================

Why this file is kept. It is the evidence for moving the published policy from
p=none to p=quarantine: 17 aggregate reports covering 28 September to 1
November, in which every source that sent mail as opendo.co.uk was Google and
nothing failed alignment. That is what makes enforcing safe, and without it the
decision is only an inference from the SPF record listing Google alone.

It is also the domain's pre-send baseline. 44 messages in five weeks is
essentially no sending history, so the reports from 10 November onward -- the
first with real traffic behind them -- are the ones worth reading, and this is
what they should be compared against.

The raw reports are not tracked. They arrive at roughly two a day once mail is
flowing, so the folder becomes hundreds of gzipped files within a year, and
they are recoverable from the DMARC label in the mailbox. Regenerate this
summary from a fresh batch with:

    python3 scripts/dmarc_summary.py docs/dmarc
